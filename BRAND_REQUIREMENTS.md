## Brand Identity

- **Brand name:** Pawmeals
- **Tagline:** Cooked Food Specialist. Natural, No Preservatives.
- **Position:** Indonesia's #1 natural dog food brand (33% market share FY2025)
- **Primary colors:** Coral/salmon red (#E05C5C), warm cream (#F5EFE6), dark charcoal text
- **Style:** Warm & Friendly, slightly formal but warm, educated & informed
- **Background:** Mostly cream / warm white — NO cool tones (no blue, no neon yellow)
- **Photography:** Happy dogs, nose-licking cues, natural ingredients, warm-tone photography
- **DO NOT:** Use low-quality images, AI-generated images, mention competitors directly
